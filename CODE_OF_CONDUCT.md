# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in `agent-tracer` to be respectful and welcoming.
Everyone should be able to contribute regardless of age, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other participants with respect and consideration.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, and accept feedback in
  good faith.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and allow others to respond.
- Respect people's privacy, time, and communication boundaries.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, threats, sexualized
language or unwanted sexual attention, and publishing someone's private
information without permission are not acceptable. Neither are repeated
disruptions, retaliation against someone who reports a concern, or attempts
to evade a moderation decision.

## Scope

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project
in public, such as through an official account or at an event.

## Reporting a concern

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer at
[junhyeok.lee@motiftech.io](mailto:junhyeok.lee@motiftech.io), the maintainer
contact also listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include what happened,
relevant links or screenshots if available, and any immediate concerns.
Do not post private details in a public issue or pull request.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as possible. Information will only be
shared with people who need it to assess and address the report. If a
maintainer is involved in a report, they must not decide its outcome; an
uninvolved maintainer should handle the review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and
restrict participation when necessary. They will consider the severity,
impact, and any pattern of behavior when deciding on a response:

1. A request to stop the behavior and correct its impact.
2. A warning that explains the violation and expected changes.
3. A temporary restriction on participation.
4. A permanent ban for severe or repeated violations.

Severe incidents may warrant an immediate restriction or ban. Maintainers
should communicate the reason and scope of a decision privately to the
affected participant when possible. Requests for reconsideration can be
sent to the same contact with additional context, for review by an
uninvolved maintainer where possible.
